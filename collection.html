A gallery of the era’s cricketers

The Collection

Cigarette and trade cards like these were a familiar sight to Emily Bardswell — pocket-sized portraits of the players whose autographs she and her contemporaries collected on bats just like the two at the heart of this story. This gallery brings together 44 cards from the period, including several of the men whose signatures appear on the Bardswell and Golden Age bats.
